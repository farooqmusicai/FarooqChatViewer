More than a text file.

01 / FIND

Return to an older message

Search conversation names and message text, then jump to a matching result inside a chat.

02 / REVISIT

Keep the media with the story

View saved photos and stickers. Play voice notes, audio and supported videos. Open documents in an installed app.

Connect your archive folder

Keep one folder per chat. Your selected library is remembered when you reopen the viewer.

Start with an export you already have.

Export a chat from WhatsApp, copy it to your PC, and choose its folder or import its ZIP. The viewer displays the messages and media included in that export.
